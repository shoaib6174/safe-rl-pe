- **Step 6 part 1+2 review fix wave** (consolidated fix of a whole-branch review; commits `d149f84..3631ca2`). Eight findings, all measurement-correctness rather than crashes. **Further definitions added to the Step 6 pre-registration, again before G1–G4 run** — all **reported, not gated**:
  - **Clearance vs barrier.** `min_h` mixed metres with metres² (obstacle barrier `dx²+dy²−r_eff²` is m², boundary barrier `x_lim−qx` is m), so the reported floor was not comparable across episodes: 0.02 m from a wall gave 0.020, the same 0.02 m from a radius-1.0 obstacle gave 0.046. **The reportable safety margin is now `min_clearance` / `min_clearance_floor`, a signed distance in metres** (obstacle: `sqrt(h + r_eff²) − r_eff`, exact; boundary: `h` unchanged). `min_h` / `min_h_floor` keep their meaning as a mixed-unit **QP margin** and must never appear in the paper as a distance; `min_h_obstacle_floor` / `min_h_boundary_floor` are filter-internal diagnostics.
  - **Immobility vs stall.** An **immobile step** = executed speed ≤ 5 % of the effective per-role cap, with **no condition on the nominal**. A **stalled step** is an immobile step that additionally had nominal ≥ 50 % of that cap. Both are reported: `stalled` is defensible ("braking a slow nominal is not the shield's fault") but is conditional on the very thing that co-adapts, so a policy that lowers its own commanded speed while pinned in a corner would report zero stalls. `immobile_step_rate` / `longest_immobile_run` are the nominal-independent numbers.
  - **Backup-tier pinning.** A **pinned episode** = ≥ 40 consecutive backup-tier steps (same threshold as deadlock). Necessary because the backup controller crawls at 10 % of the cap while the stall band is 5 %, so tier-3 immobility can never register as a stall or a deadlock. The two constants are documented as non-independent.
  - **Deadlock-threshold auditability.** The 40-step cut is now reported on a visible distribution: `longest_stall_run_per_episode`, pooled `stall_step_rate`, and a per-episode `stalled_at_episode_end` flag (a genuine force-equilibrium never recovers; a slow turn away does). Measured transients: ≈ 28 steps for a benign nose-first wall approach with a full-rate turn away, ≈ 37 for a 0.3 × ω_max turn (`FovLimitedEvader` with no bearing), so a slower learned policy can cross 40 benignly and the distribution is what separates the cases.
  - **Unshielded negative control.** Built with the new `pe2.shield.NoOpShield` through `run_shielded_duel`, reporting `method="none"` as a **distinct fourth tier** in the histogram. Its zero infeasibility rate and zero intervention rate are only interpretable alongside that tier; a hand-rolled pass-through shield is forbidden, and `summarize_shield` refuses a log with no shield block (naming `NoOpShield` in the error) and refuses a mix of shielded roles (the stall thresholds are per-role).
  - **Violation counting in the unshielded arm.** `run_duel` now counts contact steps for **both roles separately** (`pursuer_violations`, `evader_violations`, `total_violations`) — an unshielded arm has no privileged role. The shielded arm's pre-registered `violations` is unchanged: contact steps of the **shielded role**. The meaning of a violation is unchanged (an env contact flag, never a barrier sign).
  - **Feasibility has one definition.** The shield's `info["feasible"]` is the single source; `ShieldMetrics.record` reads it instead of re-deriving it from the method string, and raises on a method outside `TIERS`, so a future tier cannot vanish from the histogram while still counting toward the infeasibility rate. Pooled counts (`n_interventions`, `n_infeasible`, `n_backup`, `n_stalled`, `n_immobile`) are summed as exact integers; the rate keys keep their original pooling.
  - **`intervention_rate` is not a safety-effort proxy.** On the relaxed tier the shield returns the nominal unmodified while an obstacle barrier is negative, so a step can log no intervention while being among the least safe in the episode. Documented at the definition and pinned by a test.
  - Corrections: the two docstrings claiming `h < 0` coincides with contact were wrong — the virtual control point sits 0.1 m ahead of the body, so a barrier is a **conservative early warning** with that headroom, not a contact indicator. The backup controller now honours the effective cap (`0.1 × v_max`, not `0.1 × cfg.v_max`). `v_exec` is documented as the filter's output, which differs from the applied speed only if `prep_steps > 0` (default 0).
