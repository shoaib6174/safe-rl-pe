### Phase 3 — Hypothesis tests (single-variable CPU matchups, frozen episode_5000 nets)
Script: `repro_gonultas_isler_2024/diagnose_eval_discrepancy.py` (N=50 then N=300; results in `results_symmetric/eval_discrepancy_diagnosis{,_n300}.json`):

| Condition | Seed 9 | Seed 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Training-log mean, last 500 ep (std) | 0.468 (0.42) | 0.893 (0.23) |
| A: det, skip=1 (paper protocol) | 0.230 | 0.503 |
| B: det, skip=2 | 0.297 | 0.663 |

| E: vs episode_4750 opponent (drift proxy) | 0.33/0.26 | 0.76/0.53 |

**Root cause (three mechanisms):**
1. **Snapshot artifact**: the reported "0.500" was a single 2-episode log window; both seeds oscillate with window std 0.42/0.23. Seed 16's true training-log mean is 0.893 (pursuer-dominant) — the final window caught a downswing.
2. **Opponent non-stationarity (dominant)**: seed 9 frozen nets under the *exact* training measurement protocol (D) give 0.250 ≈ frozen-eval 0.230 — measurement protocol explains ~nothing; the inflated training-log 0.468 comes from the drifting co-adapting opponent.
3. **Measurement protocol (secondary, non-additive)**: noise alone +0.11 (s9) / +0.02 (s16); frame-skip alone +0.07 / +0.16; combined +0.02 / +0.14.

### Discovery — env resets are unseeded
- `envs/pursuit_evasion_env.py:74`: `np.random.default_rng()` (OS entropy). Every eval run draws different episode inits → N=50 evals are irreproducible. My N=50 replication of seed 9 gave 0.42 vs S115's 0.24 (pure init sampling); at N=300 seed 9 = 0.230 (matches S115's 0.24).
- Seed 16's S115 value 0.580 was Monte Carlo error: N=300 draws gave 0.503 and 0.563.
