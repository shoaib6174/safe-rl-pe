### RE produced a FOURTH pattern the pre-registration did not name
Spec §3.1 named three patterns and fixed each one's reading in advance. RE matched **none**
of them, and `anchor_pattern` correctly returned `other` with:
> verdict PRESERVED with kl_grew=True matches none of the three patterns the pre-registration
> named; report the pattern, do not force it into one of them.

What actually happened: β = 1.0 at 1M **moved AND held**. Its anchor divergence crossed the
0.001 movement bar at 400k (0.002506) and ended at **0.313685 — 313.7× the bar** — so
`kl_grew` is True and this is not a pinned actor. Yet every probe stayed at or above 0.137
and it ended at 0.140.

**The consequence for D100's open question: the dichotomy is NOT closed, and the
β-continuum reading is strengthened.** Spec §3.1 said "pinned" (moved-not-at-all) would close
it; that is not what occurred. Against T0.4's RB at β = 0.1 on the same seed and budget:

| | β = 1.0 (RE) | β = 0.1 (RB, T0.4) |
|---|---|---|
| final anchor divergence | 0.313685 (313.7× the 1e-3 bar) | 3.704653 (3704.7× the bar) |
| ratio between them | — | **11.81× RE's** |
| score trajectory | gentle decay 0.200 → 0.140, never below 0.137 | collapse after 600k: 0.105 → 0.010 → 0.000 |

So the strong anchor slows the drift by about an order of magnitude and correspondingly turns
a collapse into a slow decay — **neither strength improves on the clone**. That is the
continuum reading (anchor strength trades time-to-collapse against learning rate), now
supported by a pattern the pre-registration did not anticipate rather than by the one it did.

**What this does NOT license:** RE was still declining at its last probe (0.140, and 0.116 on
the N=500 final), so it may well collapse with more budget. That is not claimed — the budget
is fixed and the stop rule forbids extending it.

### The temperature fix worked exactly as predicted — which makes the control result real
Spec §3.2 predicted, before the run, a ±1σ weight ratio of **7.3891**. Measured: **7.3891 at
every one of RF's 15 iterations and every one of RFNEG's 5, deviation 0.0 %, no iteration
floored.** `spec_check.pass` is True for both arms. The clip fraction ran 0.00258 → 0.00442
(RF) and 0.00046 → 0.00089 (RFNEG) against a predicted ≈ 0.0014 — the same order, the
difference being that the advantage distribution is not exactly normal. λ tracked the spread
down, 0.1857 → 0.1030 (RF) and 0.1917 → 0.1591 (RFNEG), which is the point: at T0.4's fixed
λ = 0.5 the same spreads gave 2.10 falling to 1.46.

**So the weighting was genuinely engaged this time, and the control still did not
discriminate.** Spec §3.3 pre-registered exactly this case as a finding rather than a null:
> the control did not discriminate even with the weighting engaged (spec 3.3): the policy
> class is INSENSITIVE to the sign of the advantage on this task, i.e. self-imitation
> dominates the update regardless of weighting. Report this as a finding, not as another null.

At the matched 500k point the two arms read 0.165 (RF) and 0.150 (RFNEG) — preferring
better-than-expected actions and preferring worse-than-expected ones produce the same slow
decay. RF's verdict is **withheld** by the pre-registered rule; its own COLLAPSED reading must
not be cited as evidence against AWR.
