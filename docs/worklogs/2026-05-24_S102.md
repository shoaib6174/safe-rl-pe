### Pop-FSP v4 Launch — Modified Game Parameters
Launched 3-seed training with modified game to shift Nash equilibrium toward balance:

**Changes from v2/v3:**
| Parameter | v2/v3 | v4 |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| arena_size | 16.0 | 20.0 |
| evader.v_max | 1.5 | 1.75 |
| speed_ratio (pursuer/evader) | 1.67x | 1.43x |
| curriculum.evader_vmax_end | 1.5 | 1.75 |

**Rationale:** In bounded domains with partial observability, the pursuer's existential quantifier (∃: catch ANY evader) gives structural advantage. Increasing arena size reduces corner-trapping effectiveness, and increasing evader speed reduces pursuer advantage. Literature (S54) shows most PE papers give evader 10-30% speed advantage; our v4 gives evader 30% less speed than pursuer (down from 67%).
