### Pursuer Behavior Diagnosis
GIF analysis revealed pursuer doesn't chase at distance, starts slow, speeds up near capture. Root cause: **gamma=0.2** — effective horizon ~1.25 steps. Capture bonus (100.0) invisible beyond 3 steps. No urgency to close distance.

### SP15 Experiment Design
Designed comprehensive experiment batch testing:
1. **Higher gamma** (0.9 and 0.99) with scaled rewards (20/-20 and 10/-10)
2. **Stepped threshold schedule** (0.8→0.75→0.70→0.65→0.60 over 0-14M steps)
3. **Alt-freeze → pure self-play transition** at 15M steps
4. **EWC anti-forgetting** (λ=1000)
5. **Visibility reward** (w_vis_pursuer=0.1)
6. **No-obstacle control** (isolate gamma effect)

## Decisions Made
| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **Abandon asymmetric obs for self-play** | SP13k/l collapsed under full PO. Evader with full obs exploits pursuer's blind spots permanently. |
| **Abandon pure self-play from scratch** | SP14 failed. Some freeze mechanism is required for stable learning. |
| **Scale rewards with gamma** | γ=0.9→bonus 20, γ=0.99→bonus 10. Prevents Q-value explosion from longer effective horizon. |
| **Stepped threshold (0.8→0.6) not linear** | User preference. Fixed steps at 2.5M/5M/8M/11M boundaries. |
| **Self-play transition at 15M** | 50% budget for alt-freeze foundation, 50% for co-evolution. |
| **1 seed per config for exploration** | SP13 showed seed sensitivity, but exploring 10 configs > validating 5 with 2 seeds each. Validate winners later. |
| **Killed ComfyUI on niro-2** | Freed 21.4 GB VRAM. Not needed for current work. |
| **GPU-only runs (no CPU fallback)** | CPU runs 4-6x slower AND cause thread contention degrading GPU runs. |
