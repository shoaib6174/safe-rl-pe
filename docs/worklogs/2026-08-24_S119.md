### Step 6 — Safety shield (plan Step 6, in progress)

**Pre-registered gate criteria (D83 — written before any implementation, 2026-08-25):**

*Violation semantics*: a **violation** = any step where the env's kinematic contact resolution engages for the shielded role — `wall_contact` (position clip active) or `obstacle_contact` (radial projection engaged), both already reported in env info. The env makes physical penetration impossible by construction (centers held at obstacle_radius + agent_radius, walls clip at half − agent_radius); the shield's job is to act early enough that **contact never occurs**. CBF constraint radii = obs_radius + agent_radius and x_lim = half − agent_radius, so h < 0 coincides exactly with contact.

*Design (pre-registered)*: VCP-CBF discrete-time h(x_{k+1}) ≥ (1−γ)h(x_k), **γ=0.2 (D31)**, dt=0.05, VCP offset d=0.1 (root-proven params); constraints = obstacles + 4 arena boundaries, **never inter-agent on the pursuer** (capture requires contact); anisotropic least-squares QP (w_v=150, w_ω=1 — steer-don't-brake, root-proven); 3-tier fallback per `SafeActionResolver`: exact QP → relaxed QP (drop obstacle constraints, keep boundary) → backup controller (brake + turn away from nearest danger). Tier-1 SVM feasibility classifier NOT ported (unused in root practice — simplicity).

*Automated verify*:
- **G1 (kill criterion)**: shielded GreedyPursuer vs GreedyEvader, **N=1000 seeded episodes (seeds 760000+)** → **0 violation steps**. Any violation → shield math wrong; stop.
- **G2 (capture intact)**: captures must still occur; shielded capture rate ≥ 0.5 × unshielded capture rate on the same paired seeds; **paired McNemar reported as the primary comparison (D83)** with effect size (no p-threshold — descriptive; the 10pp tolerance is Step 7's exit gate, not Step 6's).
- **G3 (adversarial probe)**: new scripted `ObstacleHuggingEvader` (flees putting obstacles/boundary between itself and the pursuer, slides along them) vs shielded GreedyPursuer, **N=1000 (seeds 770000+)** → 0 violations; timeout rate reported.
- **G4 (negative control, D83)**: identical adversarial probe WITHOUT the shield → episodes-with-≥1-violation must be **≥ 5%**; if the unshielded probe produces fewer contacts, the probe is too weak — fix the probe, not the gate.
- **N justification (D83 power)**: 0/1000 → one-sided 95% upper ≈ 0.003 per-episode violation probability (rule of three); McNemar at N=1000 paired resolves few-percent discordance.
- **Seed contract extension**: 760000+ = Step 6 main verify stream; 770000+ = Step 6 adversarial probe stream (D66 lineage).

*Visual gate* — `step_06_shield.gif` (plan's 4 checklist items): nominal vs executed arrows diverge exactly at constraint approach; agent slides along obstacle boundary without contact; pursuer still makes contact-capture; intervention flash rate matches logged rate. Plus per-frame contact assertions during generation + md5-stable re-render.

*Exit*: G1–G4 pass + visual gate signed off. *Kill*: any shielded violation → stop, shield math wrong.
