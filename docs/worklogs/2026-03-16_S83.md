## Work Done

### SP15 Analysis (~38h, 19 iterations, ~2.9M steps)
- **Critical finding: No role switches occurred in any of the 10 runs.** The freeze threshold of 0.8 was unreachable under full partial observability (90° FOV, 8m range).
- All runs stayed with `frozen=evader` for their entire lifetime — effectively just pursuer training against a static initial evader.
- **Vis reward (w=0.1) was the only factor that helped**: SP15g/k achieved 0.57 avg CR vs 0.10-0.13 for all others.
- EWC had zero effect (no switches = no forgetting to prevent).
- γ=0.99 marginally better than γ=0.9 (0.13 vs 0.10) but both poor without vis reward.
- Stepped threshold schedule had no effect (SP15k = SP15g, identical trajectories).
- Masking curriculum completed successfully (p_full_obs reached 0.0 by iter 17).

### Force-Switch Feature
- Added `--force_first_switch_steps` to `training/amsdrl.py` and `scripts/train_amsdrl.py`.
- Forces first freeze role switch after N total steps regardless of capture rate.
- Ensures self-play actually begins even when threshold is unreachable.
