### Pattern Analysis
- **Pursuer lock-in**: 5/9 seeds (0, 4, 5, 6, 8)
- **Evader lock-in**: 1/9 seeds (7)
- **Pursuer-biased**: 1/9 seeds (1)
- **Balanced**: 2/9 seeds (2, 3)

The instability is **directional** — pursuer-dominant collapse is 5× more common than evader-dominant. This asymmetry persists even with symmetric dynamics, suggesting the learning dynamics (not game dynamics) introduce the bias.
