### Collapse Diagnosis & Root Cause
**All 3 seeds collapsed at self-play transition (ep 2000):**
- Pretrain: CR=1.000, Len=~50 steps, PA=-150 to -400 ✅
- Ep 2002: CR=0.000, Len=400, PA flips +672, PC explodes 13.7× (1980→27091) ❌
- By ep 2500: CR=0.000 across all seeds, no recovery

**Root cause identified:** `ema_evader` is initialized at training start as `deepcopy(evader.actor)`. During pretrain, `evader.actor` is **never updated** (evader is fixed greedy). So at transition, `ema_evader` is a **random network**. When self-play starts, the pursuer's critic uses this random EMA for target Q → immediate Q-explosion.

**The evader is NOT learning too fast** — at ep 2002, evader had only ~2 gradient updates (EA=-11, near zero). The collapse is caused by the **pursuer's Q-function being destroyed by random EMA targets**.

### Fix Implementation & Re-launch
Two-part fix committed (`effc255`):
1. **EMA re-initialization at transition**: `ema_pursuer` and `ema_evader` deep-copied from CURRENT actors at pretrain→self-play boundary
2. **Mixing curriculum (Option A)**: 1000-episode gradual rollout transition (greedy → learned evader)

**Episode structure:** 2000 pretrain + 1000 mixing + 2000 self-play = 5000 total

Re-launched on niro-2 at 10:32 UTC (PIDs 94958, 94959, 94960). Old results archived to `results_transfer_v1_0604_1032`.

## Theoretical Deep Dive

**Why self-play doesn't produce strategy cycling:**

1. **Pretrain creates overspecialization** — Pursuer learns narrow strategy against predictable greedy evader, not general pursuit skill.
2. **Asymmetric learning rates** — Evader 2× LR means it adapts faster than pursuer can recover.
3. **EMA lag** — Pursuer's Q-targets use outdated opponent, always chasing a moving target.
4. **Reward gradient asymmetry** — Winner gets strong signal, loser gets weak. Positive feedback → lock-in.
5. **Structural dynamics asymmetry** — Point-mass evader can exploit bicycle pursuer's turning constraints.
