### T3 exact-game twin — criterion MET on 3/3 layouts (commit bab287a)
7×7 gridworld from Experiment 1 with hazard cells and a projection shield; exact Nash value and BR dynamics; pre-registered threshold |ΔV| > 0.02 on ≥ 2/3 layouts.

| layout | unshielded | both shielded | Δ both | pursuer-only | Δ pursuer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sparse | 0.5748 | 0.1362 | −0.4385 | 0.1274 | −0.4474 |
| dense | 0.5603 | 0.0575 | −0.5029 | 0.0575 | −0.5029 |
| corridor | 0.5032 | 0.6127 | **+0.1096** | 0.1898 | −0.3134 |

- **Pursuer-only shielding is uniformly negative** (−0.31 to −0.50): the clean signed **R1 prediction**, and exactly the Step-8 asymmetry confound.
- **Both-shielded flips sign with geometry**: scattered hazards favour the evader, a chokepoint favours the pursuer. Jia et al. (CEP 2026, D90) assert the mechanism; this is its first measurement, and the direction dependence is new.
- Controls: hazards placed where no policy moves, and the hazard-free arm, both give Δ = 0.0 exactly; every configuration passes a unilateral-deviation saddle check with gap 0.0; both arms of a layout share identical hazards and starts, so only the projection differs. A second projection rule gives bit-identical values (only the projection's image matters to an exact optimiser), so the shift is not an artefact of the "nearest" definition.
- **Caveat for the paper**: magnitudes belong to a small grid where the shield removes much of the pursuer's mobility; the SIGN transfers, the size does not. Sanity check passes: pursuer-only is never better for the pursuer than both-shielded.
- Incidental: BR dynamics reach a 5-cycle, not a fixed point, on every layout.

- **PA**: the D82 collapse signature to the second decimal (0.185 → 0.02) once the actor unfreezes. The proven dense rewards did not prevent it. **The literal kill rule fires (35 < 50)** — but the pre-registered per-segment breakdown, added in S121 precisely to separate sparsity from collapse, shows the frozen-phase rate was 80 (> 50) and decayed in lockstep with the policy. The signal was adequate; the policy died and took the count with it.
- **PB**: passes the pass rule at every probe, and training positives stayed healthy — the anchor genuinely **prevents collapse**. But the pre-registered growth condition (A2/D95) is NOT met: anchor divergence peaked at 0.00059 against the moved threshold 0.001 (pinned ≈ 4e-6, unanchored ≈ 0.105). The runner's own words: "the anchor has preserved the clone rather than stopped drift, so a pass here is not evidence that anchored fine-tuning learns." This is the exact Critical-3 scenario the S121 final review predicted, caught by the machinery built for it. Win rates 0.205–0.215 sit inside the clone's CI, consistent with preservation.
- **PC**: start adequate (0.205 [0.155, 0.266]). Fails the rule at 100k and 150k, then **recovers to 0.140 at 200k** — the only arm whose trajectory turned around. Under the pre-registered rule this is a fail; under the pre-registered no-extension clause, a longer run would be a NEW pre-registration, not a retry.
- **CTRL**: collapsing faster than D78 (0.010 by 50k with no warm-up). At ~1/200 the Wilson upper bound is ≈ 0.028 < 0.137, so the collapse will certify and the harness validates.
