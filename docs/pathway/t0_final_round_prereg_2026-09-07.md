## 1. Why this round exists, and why it is a *fresh* pre-registration

The T0.4 round (D100) closed with 0 IMPROVED across three learners. Its own final review
then established that two cells of the argument were never actually observed:

- **The strong anchor was never run at the full budget.** Tier 0's β = 1.0 arm ran 200,000
  steps; T0.4's β = 0.1 arm ran 1,000,000. They differ in budget as well as in anchor
  strength, so "the strong anchor cannot learn" is not in evidence — only "it had not crossed
  the movement threshold within 200k".
- **The advantage-weighted arm never exercised its own mechanism.** At the pre-registered
  temperature λ = 0.5, against a measured advantage spread of 0.186 decaying to 0.095, the
  weight ratio between a one-sigma-good and one-sigma-bad action was only 2.10× falling to
  1.46×. Treatment and control produced indistinguishable curves (Fisher p ≈ 0.69), the
  control did not collapse, and the arm was correctly **withheld, not refuted**.

Both candidate follow-ups were therefore chosen *because of* what T0.4 showed. That makes
them hypotheses generated by the data, and testing a data-generated hypothesis on the data
that generated it establishes nothing. Hence a fresh document, fresh seed streams, and the
full build-review-run cycle again. λ was NOT retuned inside T0.4 when the flat weights were
seen; it is being re-specified here, before any run, with its predicted effect stated in
advance so the specification is itself falsifiable (§3.2).

## 2. Common protocol (binding)

- Role: pursuer. Opponent: frozen `GreedyEvader`. Environment `EnvConfig()` defaults.
- Budget **1,000,000 env steps** per learner arm; probes every **100,000** steps, N = 200.
- **Final probe** at 1,000,000 steps, N = **500**.
- **Eval stream: 840000+** (fresh; 700000–790999, 800000–810999, 820000+, 830000+ are
  consumed by Steps 5–6, Tier 0 and T0.4; 900000+ is the test range).
- **The start bar is re-measured on this round's own stream.** T0.4's bar (37/200 = 0.185)
  came from the D76 battery on stream 720000+ while its probes ran on 780000+ — flagged in
  the T0.4 final review as a mismatch that happened to disfavour the arms. Here the gated
  clone `results/visual_gates/step_05/sac_bc_pursuer.zip` is evaluated ONCE, deterministically,
  at N = 500 on seeds 840000–840499, before any training begins, and that number is
  `START_500`, recorded in the round JSON with its Wilson interval. Every arm is judged
  against it, and every probe is paired with it by construction.
- **Pass rule (no collapse):** win rate ≥ **0.137** (`CLONE_CI_LO`, the gated clone's Wilson
  lower bound) at EVERY probe from **500,000** steps onward. A missing probe in the window →
  `UNDETERMINED` with a worded reason.
- **Improvement rule:** the final N = 500 probe's Wilson **lower** bound (z = 1.96) exceeds
  `START_500`'s point estimate. Both are measured on the same 500 seeds from the same clone.
- **Verdicts:** `COLLAPSED` / `PRESERVED` / `IMPROVED` / `UNDETERMINED`, as in D99 §C2.
  **Survivor = IMPROVED.**
- Training seeds: RE **7** (A1's off-policy seed, as Tier 0's P-B and T0.4's RB used),
  RF **13**, RF-neg **13** (paired; they differ only in the advantage sign).
- Learner recipe unchanged and binding: LayerNorm critics, fixed `ent_coef = 1e-5`,
  critic warm-up 50,000 (D78/D80/D81).
- **No budget extension, no criterion change, no seed re-roll, and no temperature change
  after any number of this round is seen.**

## 3. The arms

### 3.1 RE — the untested anchor cell: β = 1.0 at 1M
`finetune_br_anchored`, identical to Tier 0's P-B in every respect except the budget
(200,000 → 1,000,000). Reports the A2 anchor diagnostic (`anchor_kl` per probe,
`anchor_kl_grew` against `ANCHOR_KL_MOVED = 1e-3`) alongside its verdict.

**The three outcome patterns and what each means are fixed now**, so the interpretation is
not chosen after the fact. The verdict itself is decided by §2's rules; this table governs
what the round *concludes about the mechanism*:

| pattern | reading, pre-committed |
|---|---|
| **Pinned**: holds ≥ 0.137 throughout, `anchor_kl` never crosses 1e-3 | The strong anchor genuinely cannot learn even given 5× the budget. Together with RB's collapse at β = 0.1 this **does** close the dichotomy that D100 correctly refused to claim on the evidence then available. |
| **Late collapse**: holds, then falls, with `anchor_kl` running away as RB's did after 600k | Supports the **β-continuum** reading: anchor strength trades time-to-collapse against learning rate, and no strength escapes. Route C's claim strengthens from "these settings failed" to "the mechanism has no operating point". |
| **IMPROVED** | A survivor. β = 1.0 at 1M is the Track-1 learner and Track 2 is planned for it. |

No new negative control: RE runs inside SB3's own loop on the probe machinery that Tier 0's
CTRL already validated by collapsing (0.010, Wilson upper 0.036).

### 3.2 RF — the untested AWR cell: a temperature that keeps the weighting engaged
`train_awr` with one change, and one only: the temperature becomes **adaptive**,
`λ_t = max(std(A_t), 0.01)` computed per iteration over that iteration's buffer, replacing the
fixed λ = 0.5. Everything else is the T0.4 configuration verbatim (`w_max = 20`, γ = 0.999,
300 initial episodes, FIFO 300, ≥ 50,000 fresh steps per iteration, value net 256-256-1 at
Adam 1e-3 × 20 epochs, actor at Adam 3e-4 × 10 epochs).

**Why adaptive rather than a smaller fixed number.** The advantage spread is not stationary:
it decayed monotonically from 0.1857 to 0.0953 across T0.4's fifteen iterations as the value
network learned to predict its own returns. Any fixed λ chosen for the early spread is too
large late, and one chosen for the late spread saturates the clip early. Dividing by the
measured spread makes the weight distribution scale-free, so the mechanism is exercised
identically at every iteration regardless of how well the value net fits.

**The specification's own predicted diagnostics, stated in advance** (standardised advantages,
so weights are `exp(Â)` with Â of unit spread):

| quantity | T0.4 measured (λ = 0.5) | RF predicted (λ_t = std) |
|---|---|---|
| weight ratio, ±1σ | 2.10 → 1.46 | **7.39**, constant across iterations |
| weight ratio, ±2σ | 4.42 → 2.14 | **54.6**, clipped at 20 |
| `weight_clipped_fraction` | 0 (max 1.6e-5) | **≈ 0.0014** |

If the observed diagnostics do not match these predictions, the *implementation* is wrong and
the arm is `UNDETERMINED` on those grounds — not evidence about AWR. This is deliberate: it
makes the temperature specification falsifiable separately from the learning hypothesis, which
is exactly what T0.4 lacked. Rollout stream **850000+**.

### 3.3 RF-neg — the control, which now does double duty
`train_awr` with `advantage_sign = -1.0` and the same adaptive temperature, 500,000 steps
(the C3-a budget, so the offline phase's ~306k is followed by four online iterations),
rollout stream **860000+**, probes every 100,000.

**It must collapse** by `control_collapsed` (final probe's Wilson upper bound < 0.137). If it
does not, RF is **withheld** exactly as RD was. But this control now tests two things at once:

- that the probe path and the non-SB3 training loop measure what they claim; and
- **that the weighting is doing the work.** With an engaged weighting, preferring the
  worse-than-expected action must degrade the policy. A second failure to discriminate — with
  the weight spread confirmed at 7.4× — would no longer be uninformative: it would be a
  **reportable finding** that this policy class is insensitive to the sign of the advantage on
  this task, i.e. that self-imitation dominates the update regardless of weighting. Record it
  as such rather than as another null.

## 7. Known limitation this round does NOT close

The β continuum is sampled at two points only (1.0 and 0.1), at one budget each after this
round. If RE shows late collapse, the continuum reading is *supported*, not established; no
intermediate β is tested and none will be. That limitation belongs in the paper as written
here, and is not grounds for a further round (§5).
