## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1: section_completeness
Every section of the approved outline (abstract, 1–8, the Generative AI Use subsection, the reproducibility statement) is written in full. No placeholder survives except the one the user owns (the corresponding author's email, marked [to confirm]).

### D2: citation_density
Every claim about prior work carries a citation from the verified list, and every empirical claim is backed by one of the paper's own archived records (a round JSON named in the evidence map). Claims that have neither are rewritten as hedged statements or removed. No citation is introduced that is not on the verified list.

### D3: argument_blueprint_fidelity
Each section opens with the claim the skeleton assigns to it, cites the record or source the evidence map assigns, and closes by linking back to the section's purpose. The pre-registered verdicts bound every claim: a FAIL is written as the skeleton's consequence sentence (scoped, "typically", "cannot leave the trap", "present but weaker"), never as a pass.

### D4: total_word_count
Total text stays within ±10 % of 5 200 words (4 680–5 720), measured without captions, tables and the bibliography.

### D5: per_section_word_count
Each section stays within ±15 % of its skeleton allocation (abstract 180, intro 750, related work 500, setting 550, formal 900, experiments 1 350, dissociations 450, discussion 400, conclusion + reproducibility 230).

### D6: paragraph_structure
Body paragraphs lead with a topic sentence, give the evidence (a number with its N or a citation), explain what it shows, and link to the next step. Paragraph lengths vary. Section transitions are explicit sentences, not headings alone.

### D7: register_consistency
Formal technical register throughout. No colloquial phrasing, no AI-typical filler terms, at most one em dash in the whole paper, semicolons rare, no sentences that describe what the section is about to do. The word "pinned" is not used. Captions carry no rate that is not in a table.
