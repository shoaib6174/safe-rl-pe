\section{Training details}

\paragraph{Observation.} The head's base observation has 11 components: own position $x/5$, $y/5$ and heading $\theta/\pi$; the evader's relative position in the body frame divided by 10 and relative heading divided by $\pi$, all zero when the evader is not visible; a visibility flag; and the centres of the two obstacles in the body frame divided by 10. Obstacle radii are not observed; walls are seen only through the own position. The three filter features are the intervention flag of the last executed step, the minimum clearance clipped to $[-0.5, 2]$\,m, and the stalled-step run divided by 40 and clipped to $[0, 2]$ (constant after 80 stalled steps). The two allowed-turn features of \Obs\ and \ObsHor\ are the admissible turn-rate range at zero speed at the current pose. Widths: \Free\ 11; \Base, \Hor, \Aware\ 14; \Obs, \ObsHor\ 16.

\paragraph{Reward and discount.} $+1$ on capture and $-1$ on timeout; policies trained behind the filter pay $10^{-3}$ per stalled step inside a learner transition (the penalty family of Experiment~5 pays $10^{-2}$). One learner transition is up to 10 held head steps followed by the intercept law's run until it hands back; in training a transition lasted 14.0--16.6 simulation steps on average (for example 15.9 for \Base\ seed 1). The discount applies per transition.

\paragraph{PPO.} Stable-Baselines3 2.7.1 with its defaults except the discount: MLP policy with two hidden layers of 64 tanh units, 2\,048 steps per rollout, minibatch 64, 10 epochs, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, GAE $\lambda = 0.95$, clip 0.2, entropy coefficient 0. The recurrent arm of Experiment~5 used RecurrentPPO with an LSTM policy. The trained families ran 5\,M simulation steps, \Free\ and \Aware\ 10\,M. \Free\ is the best of five seeds on its confirmation evaluation (capture 0.738 there); \Aware\ is the best of the three seeds of the remedy with both filter features and the stall penalty.

\paragraph{Intercept law.} Chase: full speed with $\omega = \mathrm{clip}(3\,\beta)$ for bearing $\beta$; go to the remembered point at full speed until within \SI{0.5}{m}. The head's hold ends early when the evader becomes visible.
